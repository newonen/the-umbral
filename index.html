The Umbral

The Rift is open.

An anime card game that lives in your Discord server. Summon heroes from eleven series, build a team of three and climb into the dark with Umbra, who will judge every pull you make.

"Free to play. Free to lose. I take no payment, only amusement."

Add Umbra to your server Join the community

What you do in the Rift

Everything happens with slash commands or short , commands, right in the chat.

Summon

A free daily pull, Common Packs every level, and Basic, Premium and Legendary Packs paid with in-game Gold. Cards go from R to LR, and LRs ascend to D5.

Build a team

Seven roles: Tanks, Supports, Debuffers and four kinds of damage. Every character has its own ultimate, passive and power.

Climb the Hunt

Eleven locations, eighteen floors each: duo bosses, an LR in the middle and another at the top. Every first clear pays Gold.

Raid together

Spawn a raid boss, invite friends or open it to everyone, and split the rewards with the group.

Clans

Create a clan, recruit with your clan ad, level it up and share the treasury.

Trade and the Codex

Trade cards with other players, sell on the market, and collect every character and skin in the Codex.

Eleven series, one Rift

Jujutsu Kaisen, Tensura, The Eminence in Shadow, Naruto, Dragon Ball, Bleach, One Piece, Demon Slayer, Hunter x Hunter, Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood and Black Clover.

Free, forever

Nothing is for sale

The Umbral is free. There are no purchases, no paid packs, no premium currency and no advantage you can buy. Gold, cards and every other item exist only inside the game and have no real-money value.

In the future we may accept voluntary donations. If we do, they will only help pay for the tools that keep the game running: the server that hosts the bot and the AI tools we use to build it and make its art. A donation will never give in-game items, rewards or any advantage.
