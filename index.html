The Umbral

The Rift is open.

An anime card game that lives in your Discord server. Summon 144 heroes from 14 anime worlds, build a team of three and climb into the dark with Umbra, who will judge every pull you make.

"Free to play. Free to lose. I take no payment, only amusement."

What you do in the Rift

Everything happens with slash commands or short , commands, right in the chat.

Summon

A free daily pull, Common Packs every level, and Basic, Premium and Legendary Packs paid with in-game Gold. Cards go from R to LR, and LRs ascend to D5.

Build a team

Seven roles: Tanks, Supports, Debuffers and four kinds of damage. Every character has its own skills, mechanic and a build made for the Arena or for PvE.

Climb the Hunt

14 locations, eighteen floors each: duo bosses, an LR in the middle and another at the top. Every first clear pays Gold.

Raid together

Spawn a raid boss with shared HP, test your team on it, then call friends or open it to everyone. Every player brings their own share of the drops.

Ranked Arena

Teams of five, stats evened out so skill counts. Pick who to ban, keep a card on the bench that boosts your team and jumps in when someone falls, and take revenge on whoever raids your defense. Six divisions, Arena Medals and a season skin.

Event Towers

Seasonal dungeons you climb floor by floor, with a boss every five floors, their own shop and rare skins.

Clans

Create a clan with players from every server, recruit with your clan ad, check in every day, clear the daily clan missions to level it up and spend Rift Marks in the weekly Clan Shop.

Market and Codex

Trade cards, sell on the Global Market and collect every character and skin in the Codex. One account follows you to every server.

Community Giveaway

The small tax on market sales fills a community pot. When it is full, a giveaway opens and the winners split it. All of it goes back to the players.

See it in the chat

Every fight plays out as an animated battle right in the channel, on a battlefield of its own.

14 worlds, one Rift

New worlds keep tearing through the Rift. Every series brings its own heroes, an LR to chase and a Hunt location to clear.

Free, forever

Nothing is for sale

The Umbral is free. There are no purchases, no paid packs, no premium currency and no advantage you can buy. Gold, cards and every other item exist only inside the game and have no real-money value.

In the future we may accept voluntary donations. If we do, they will only help pay for the tools that keep the game running: the server that hosts the bot and the AI tools we use to build it and make its art. A donation will never give in-game items, rewards or any advantage.
