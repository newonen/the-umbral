Beginner's guide

From zero to the Arena

Ten steps, about five minutes. Every command works as a slash command (type / and pick one) or as a short comma shortcut. The shortcuts are shown here.

"You know nothing. Perfect. That is my favourite kind of player."

1 · Add the bot2 · Start3 · Summon4 · Your cards5 · Team6 · Hunt7 · Get stronger8 · Raids9 · Arena10 · Everything else

  1. Add Umbra to your server

    Invite the bot with the button below, or join the community server and play there. Your account is global: the same cards, Gold and progress follow you to every server.

    Add Umbra to your serverJoin the community

  2. Claim your Welcome Pack

    Use /start. You get a free Welcome Pack: 10 cards with one LR guaranteed, plus a free Premium Pack for later.

    /start begin your journey,guide this guide, inside Discord

  3. Summon every day

    The daily summon is a free 5-card multi that resets at 00:00 UTC. Never skip it. Gold buys bigger packs: Basic (25k, at least one SR), Premium (50k, at least one UR) and Legendary (200k, no R at all).

    ,s d free daily summon,shop every pack, with buy buttons

    Rarities, from weak to wow: R · SR · UR · LR. Too many R cards? Merge 20 R into an SR, 15 SR into a UR, 10 UR into an LR.

  4. Look at your cards

    Every card has a #number, and that is how you point at it in commands. Each card has a role (Tank, Support, Debuffer or one of four damage types) and an element. Water beats Fire, Electric beats Water, Stone beats Electric, Grass beats Stone, Fire beats Grass.

    ,i your inventory,c #12 open card #12,cx gojo the Codex entry of any character

  5. Build your team

    A team has 3 cards. Team 1 is for the Hunt, team 2 for Raids and team 3 for Dungeons (2 and 3 copy team 1 while empty). A good first team is one Tank or Support plus two damage cards, highest level first.

    ,t see and edit your team,t 1 #12 #5 #8 set team 1 in one go

    Two or three characters from the same group (Straw Hats, Hashira, Shadow Garden…) get a bonus together. The Codex shows each character's groups.

  6. Clear the Hunt

    The Hunt is the main road: 14 locations with 18 floors each, duo bosses, an LR in the middle and another at the top. Every first clear pays 5,000 Gold, and finishing a location pays 50,000 Gold and 50,000 XP. Each fight costs 5 stamina; you start with room for 60 (+2 every player level) and get 1 back every 10 minutes.

    ,h fight the next floor,h all spend all your stamina

  7. Get stronger

    Fights drop XP Fragments. Spend them, or feed spare cards to the ones you use. A copy of the same character and rarity adds a star (UR up to D3, LR up to D5). Both show a preview first: nothing is used until you press Confirm.

    ,lvlup #12 max level up with fragments,lvlup #12 feed all feed spare R and SR cards,dupe #12 max ascend with every free copy,merge r merge R cards into an SR

  8. Raid with friends

    Spawn a boss with shared HP and beat it together. You get one Raid Pass every 4 hours (up to 10 saved). The boss shows up in your DMs first: press 🧪 Raid Test to see your damage, or 📣 Call Raids to ping players with the @Raids role. Press Auto and your team attacks by itself. Groups earn more Gold each and finish the big bosses in time; drops are the same per player.

    ,rd spawn e easy boss (or h hard, i impossible),rd see your raid,rd test test your team on the boss

  9. Climb the Ranked Arena

    Real players, stats evened out so the better team wins, not the richer one. Your Arena team has 5 cards and it is also your defense:

    • Slots 1 to 3 fight.
    • Slot 4 is the bench: it boosts your team from the back and jumps in at full HP when someone falls.
    • Slot 5 is the reserve, used when one of your cards is banned.

    Before each fight you scout the rival and ban one of their cards. Lose your defense? Take revenge. You get 1 ticket every 3 hours (up to 5, full again at midnight UTC), climb six divisions and earn Arena Medals and a season skin.

    ,ar your Arena status,ar team build it by hand or Auto-build,ar fight scout, ban, fight

  10. Everything else

    Event Towers

    Seasonal dungeons, a boss every five floors, their own shop and rare skins.

    ,dg

    Clans

    Join players from every server, level the clan up and share its treasury.

    ,clan

    Market

    Buy and sell cards with everyone. The sales tax fills the community giveaway.

    ,mk

    Your profile

    Level, collection and PvP record. Pick your best card to show it off.

    ,profile
